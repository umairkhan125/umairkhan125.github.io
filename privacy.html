Privacy Policy

Muse Uploader · Last updated 22 October 2026

1. Summary

Muse Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner. It does not collect data from the public, has no user accounts of its own, and does not sell or share any data.

2. Data the app uses

  • TikTok access tokens – issued by TikTok when the owner connects an account. Used only to upload videos to that account.
  • Basic TikTok profile info (display name, avatar, open ID) – used only to show which account a video is going to.
  • Videos and captions – the owner's own files, sent to TikTok when uploading.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer and private backup. Nothing is stored on a public server.

4. Sharing

Data is sent only to TikTok, through its official API, to perform the upload. It is never sold, rented or shared with anyone else, and is not used for advertising or analytics.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. To revoke access, go to TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions and remove Muse Uploader; stored tokens are then deleted.

6. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
